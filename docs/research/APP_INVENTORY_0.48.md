# The 7th Sense — what the app has (0.48, 2026-11-08) — brief for the market review

Web PWA (React), Hebrew + English, Web MIDI (Chrome/Android) + microphone pitch (YIN) + chroma. Supabase accounts.

**Ear & theory:** 115 topic generators (all ≥ 3 levels; random key / tempo so nothing is memorised): intervals (incl.
compound, singing intervals), degrees (major / minor / chromatic), chord quality, 7ths, inversions, extensions, voicings,
cadences, progressions, turnarounds, modulation, chromatic harmony (N6, aug6, borrowed), harmonic dictation, two-voice,
bass dictation, staff dictation (write what you hear), rhythm dictation / reading / tapping, meter, odd meters, swing
feel, syncopation, polyrhythm (+ tapping both hands), inner clock (gap click), tempo memory, form (AABA, rondo,
sequences), texture, style periods, mood, dynamics / articulation, ornaments, timbre / producer's ear (EQ, reverb, pan,
width, compression), maqam (quarter tones) & iqa'at, set classes, counterpoint, four-part writing, Roman numerals,
key signatures, spelling, circle of fifths, chord-scale, transposition, Nashville numbers, audiation. Barry Harris: 12
topics (6th-diminished, conversions, brothers, pivots, lines, block chords, "same notes other names"). Jazz ear:
guide-tone lines, comping rhythms.
**Learning path:** adaptive themed units, goals (general, sing, jazz, read, guitar, producer, exam, maqam), level test
(placement), ✓ by evidence, daily workout, spaced review, skill map (24 skills, 7 areas), ranks Bronze→Master.
**Lessons:** 139 mini-lessons (spoken TTS + examples + staff), each with a short core + "In depth"; glossary 137 terms.
**Games:** Echo Chain, Beat Echo, True or Bluff, ghost race, duels (online / same device), daily challenge (share
squares), weekly puzzle, live rooms, tournaments, voice-steered flight, rhythm highway, melody puzzle, tune-up (by ear),
name that tune, pitch memory, peak hunter, rush games (chord / note / fretboard / interval / key sig / progression),
classroom bingo.
**Voice:** range, holds, glides, leaps, melody, scales, staccato, messa di voce, 11 vibrato kinds, harmony singing,
Barry Harris singing, sight-singing, ornaments & dynamics, warm-up room, take recordings + then-and-now, precise
feedback (scoop, drift, wobble).
**Piano:** technique families (scales, arpeggios, cadences, Hanon / five-finger, control: articulation & dynamics &
voicing, advanced: octaves / double notes / polyrhythm, Barry Harris 13 levels, jazz lines, LH voicings 17 levels,
accompaniment: Alberti, walking, boogie, Latin, pop, stride 10 levels); every progression as a drill: 13 voicing types
(shells, guide, rootless A/B, close, drop 2/3, open, two-handed, quartal, upper structure, block) × 6 comping rhythms ×
LH-comps-RH-improvises; tempo ladder; per-note ✓/✗; MIDI velocity / pedal analysis.
**Guitar / bass:** tuner, neck & key notes, CAGED, chord shapes, scales, technique families (fretting, picking, legato,
scales, arps, chords, rhythm by genre, lead: bends / vibrato / slides, fingerstyle, flamenco, jazz, reading, impro,
bass), one-minute changes, chord ring-check (which strings rang).
**Songs & pieces:** 40 public-domain songs (melody / chords / both / sing); My pieces (MusicXML / MIDI import → the coach:
chunks, accuracy-gated tempo ladder, step back to the error bar, seams, joins, interleaving, day warm-ups — research
based); drill creator.
**Improvisation:** Charts (type chords → backing in 6 styles, any key / tempo; 14 templates, 14 graded improvisation
levels up to Coltrane changes, 12 standards' changes); improvise by mic/MIDI under rules — a scale per bar (set by
player / teacher), chord tones only, guide tones on 1 & 3, land on the 3rd, three notes, steps / thirds only, continuous
eighths, triplets, quarters, 2 bars on 2 off — scored note by note with a bar map; call & response.
**Tools:** tuner, pendulum metronome (49 grooves, 6 drum kits, speed trainer), drone, slow-down & A–B loop of own audio,
practice log, mixer (per channel EQ / comp / chorus / reverb / delay).
**Social & teaching:** friends, profile, leagues (weekly, tiers Root→13), duels, live rooms, duet streaks, nudges,
classes (teacher: assignments of lessons / levels, roster), push reminders, privacy / young-player mode.
**Missing / known gaps:** no real score view (lead sheet with cursor), teacher assignments of pieces + dashboards,
audio (polyphonic) following, PDF import, native apps / iOS MIDI, video, licensed songs, payments.
