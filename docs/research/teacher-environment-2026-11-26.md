# Teacher environment: how comparable products design it

Research date: 2026-11-26. Public pages only (vendor sites, help centres, App Store listings, teacher blogs and podcasts, review aggregators). Where a source is vendor marketing or a single review, that is marked. Some products had no public teacher-side information; that is reported as a gap, not filled in.

---

## 0. Two market signals before the detail

1. **The two best-known "teacher + practice app" products for private studios left the market.**
   - **Tonara** (the reference studio practice app) shut down; teachers had to migrate. Sources: https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode132/ , https://www.leilaviss.com/blog/transition-from-tonara-to-practice-space , https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode085/
   - **Yousician's teacher programme** has ended ("the Yousician for Teachers program has come to an end", per its schools page as surfaced in search: https://yousician.com/schools ; page returned 403 to direct fetch). A review site describes Yousician as having "limited emphasis on teacher assignment and multi-student dashboards" (https://wifitalents.com/best/piano-lesson-software/).
   - Lesson: a self-teaching game with a bolted-on teacher layer did not hold; the surviving teacher products are small, teacher-built tools (Practice Space, Vivid Practice, MetaPractice, Better Practice) or full school LMSs (SmartMusic, MusicFirst, Flat). Migration of a teacher's assignment library was the single most praised thing when Tonara closed, so **teacher data portability (import/export of library) is a trust feature**.
2. **Gamification is divisive among private teachers.** Several teachers explicitly chose Vivid Practice *because* it has no points; Practice Space's reward system is listed as a con ("some students may focus on rewards over practice"). Sources: https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode132/ , https://colourfulkeys.ie/piano-practice-apps/

## 1. Music-specific products

### 1.1 Tonara (closed) — private studio practice app
Sources: https://topmusic.co/6-ways-to-save-time-with-tonara-in-your-piano-studio/ , https://www.leilaviss.com/blog/transition-from-tonara-to-practice-space , https://www.148apps.com/app/1205274433/ , https://apps.appfollow.io/android/tonara-manage-motivate-music-students-to-play/com.tonara.fame?country=us
- **Dashboard / roster:** digital studio of students, calendar of events; real-time view of practice time and assignment completion per student ("let them know whether a student had warmed up and start lessons on problem areas"); weekly/monthly/yearly practice views.
- **Assignments:** reusable assignments with embedded resources (sheet music, backing tracks, theory games, videos, voice notes); a central media library; teacher could record a quick audio snippet in the lesson and attach it (the most-missed feature after the shutdown). Complaint: a set time had to be assigned *per assignment*, which one teacher "DETESTED".
- **Groups:** students grouped by lesson day, exam entry, level or method, to message in one click or run group projects.
- **Feedback / comms:** message centre with audio messages — "my favorite thing to do in Tonara" (Leila Viss). Video recording only from phone (complaint).
- **Motivation:** mic-based practice detection awarding points for length and consistency; studio and global leaderboards; custom studio leaderboards; stickers.
- **Parents:** could verify practice duration.
- **Weakness:** practice counted only while Tonara was in the foreground (switching to forScore stopped the clock).

### 1.2 Practice Space — private studio (teacher app + student app + web)
Sources: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1477106977 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/practice-space/id1451645821 , https://colourfulkeys.ie/piano-practice-apps/ , https://www.leilaviss.com/blog/transition-from-tonara-to-practice-space
- **Lesson = unit of work:** teacher creates a lesson with demonstration videos, practice goals and lesson notes; notes (rich text) also used for announcements and repertoire tracking; notes can be copied forward week to week; families who prefer email get weekly lesson notes.
- **Assignment library:** search bar + tag cloud — described as "transformative"; bulk upload by spreadsheet; share tasks with colleagues. Complaints: library save must happen before publishing or the option is lost; no bulk tagging; too many tags = scrolling; extra clicks per assignment.
- **Time model:** *one* suggested daily practice time instead of time per assignment (praised).
- **Recordings / feedback:** students record in app; teacher comments on or "likes" uploads in a Video Feed. Complaint: no audio-only messages, teacher must answer video by video or comment; audio cannot be recorded straight into an assignment; desktop video processing slow.
- **Engagement:** weekly studio leaderboard, points, gems, badges, certificates, avatars, teacher-created "In-Studio Rewards" redeemed at the next lesson; student challenges.
- **Comms:** groups with messaging; announcements with scheduling, pinning and **read tracking**.
- **Add-on "Creative Suite":** score editor, AI-generated practice pieces, speech-to-notes dictation, **AI-generated assignments from lesson notes**.
- **Multi-teacher studios:** supported ("many features for multi-teacher studios").
- **Pricing:** flat ~$9.99/month, unlimited students and storage.
- **Reviews:** "such a time saver"; developers "SO quick to respond"; wishes: lesson reminders, payment tracking; "a couple bugs".
- **Muzie:** no public source linked Muzie to Practice Space or described a Muzie teacher product (gap).

### 1.3 Vivid Practice (Vibrant Music Teaching) — private studio, deliberately non-gamified
Sources: https://colourfulkeys.ie/piano-practice-apps/ , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6450007616 , https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode132/
- Statistics section showing each student's practice **frequency** at a glance; **Future / Active** tabs for planning assignments ahead.
- Assignments with PDFs, recordings, videos; personal library + curated "Vivid Collection" + bulk upload; reorder, filter, **print as PDF** (valued for families who want paper).
- Teacher chooses which of four practice modes each student may use (Blocked, Interleaved, Focused, Pomodoro) — a rare *practice-method* control per student.
- Messaging inside groups and assignments. Tonara import (assignments tagged [T]).
- No rewards. Teachers praised "simplicity", "few clicks needed during lessons". Complaints: no laptop access for students, sending audio clunky. Free single-student account for trying.

### 1.4 MetaPractice Teacher — goal-structured private studio
Source: https://apps.apple.com/app/id1511591780 , https://colourfulkeys.ie/piano-practice-apps/
- Per-student "goalbooks" with categories/subcategories, media, notes.
- Goal types: **one-time task, repeating drill, recurring activity**; goals flagged trouble or bonus with severity; unchecked goals flagged for reassignment.
- Student level (Beginner/Skilled/Advanced) changes how goals display.
- **Repertoire list** with rules for including old pieces in routines (review/maintenance).
- **Auto-generated practice routine** per student from level, goals, repertoire; teacher can **simulate the student's session** (preview as student).
- Templates and automated recurring assignments; audio scratchpad in lesson; chat with media; events/recitals with programme reports.
- Weaknesses: 250 MB storage; no groups or multi-teacher; setup time.

### 1.5 Better Practice — web, analytics-heavy
Sources: https://www.betterpracticeapp.com/ , https://colourfulkeys.ie/piano-practice-apps/
- 23,000+ pre-entered assignments; roadmap view of method-book series for planning; **spaced-repetition "smart" daily practice lists** and a Review List.
- Detailed analytics (frequency, duration, completion); icons showing which practice aids (e.g. metronome) were used; charts and journals.
- Substitute teachers; groups; announcements; chat within each assignment; social-style newsfeed; leaderboard of who practised today.
- Parents: notification when the child starts practising; weekly summaries.
- Recording upload is a paid add-on (con); higher per-student price; web only; "may feel overwhelming".

### 1.6 Practice Prescriptions, Practice Now!, MuseClass (small)
- Practice Prescriptions: structured assignments, library for reuse — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1669147867
- Practice Now!: practice tools + "review systems for teachers" — https://apps.apple.com/au/app/practice-now/id1612123384
- MuseClass: students turn in video or audio recordings — https://apps.apple.com/us/app/museclass-student-assignments/id1535821824

### 1.7 SmartMusic / MakeMusic Cloud — school ensembles (band/orchestra/choir)
Sources: https://www.makemusic.com/blog/pro-tips-for-creating-assignments-in-makemusic-cloud/ , https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/2516/feed_items/8179802 , https://tagteam.harvard.edu/hub_feeds/2516/feed_items/12142435 , https://makemusic.com/?p=40280 , https://smartmusic.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026370553 , https://www.commonsense.org/education/reviews/smartmusic/teacher-reviews/3980916
- **First screen:** Home with class cards; each card links to Practice (Class Analytics).
- **Assignment flow:** start from a piece (catalogue / My Library / uploaded notation / Sight Reading Builder) → Assign → directions → assessment tolerance (default Level 1, "keep it there until students need a greater challenge") → **tempo "at least" X** → grading: auto score, ungraded, or custom rubric → settings. **Every assignment becomes a template**; **Units** assign several at once in order. Grading periods made optional in 2023 (teachers disliked being forced).
- **Rubrics:** custom criteria (title, description, points), reusable; examples: musicality, evidence of practice, reflection.
- **Gradebook:** recordings + auto-assessment side by side; teacher adds criteria values and comments, validates, can reopen, reassign, download recordings; export grades to any LMS.
- **Class Analytics:** time spent recording, how often material opened; MakeMusic itself warns tracked time undercounts real practice.
- **Student options:** hide coloured feedback while practising; save takes as .wav; "submit the take you're most proud of and self-analyse it".
- **Complaints (MakeMusic's own forum + reviews):** new version a "step backward"; "engineers ... have no idea how or why we used the program"; beginners discouraged by auto-assessment grades ("ready to give up"); assessment grades going straight to report cards; some method-book assignments skip instruments. Forum thread "Smartmusic grading is awful": https://help.makemusic.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/360038587234-Smartmusic-grading-is-awful (403 on direct fetch; content from search snippets).

### 1.8 MusicFirst Classroom + PracticeFirst — school LMS for music
Sources: https://www.musicfirst.com/software/musicfirst-classroom , https://www.musicfirst.com/musicfirst-2024-updates , https://www.musicfirst.com/blog/five-reasons-use-practicefirst-your-ensembles , https://sites.google.com/glcomets.net/beagle-band/musicfirst-and-playing-assignment-faqs
- Central hub: integrated software, **student portfolios**, gradebook, communication.
- "Items to Grade" moved under a **bell icon** in class tabs (2024); separate "Recent Activity" alerts removed — consolidation of to-grade signals into one place.
- PracticeFirst: performance assignments auto-assessed for rhythm, pitch, intonation, duration; rigor adjustable Easy→Hard; works for polyphonic instruments and voice.
- District FAQ: technique/scale assignments are graded **fully by the teacher**, not auto-scored — auto-assessment is not trusted for everything.

### 1.9 Flat for Education — notation/composition + performance assignments
Sources: https://help.flat.io/en/education/review-assignments-activities/ , https://help.flat.io/en/education/feedback-assignments-activities/ , https://blog.flat.io/track-and-boost-music-student-progress/ , https://help.flat.io/en/education/student-view-for-teachers , https://blog.flat.io/performance-assignments-just-got-an-upgrade-more-tools-flexibility/
- Assignment dashboard (class progress) → open assignment → "students' work" per student.
- **Watch students work live**; comments **anchored to a spot in the score**, students reply inline.
- Grade in platform, sync to Google Classroom; **Return** sends work back; **Reset student work** for a fresh attempt; **submission history**.
- Worksheets auto-graded or manual review before release.
- **Student Preview**: see the assignment as the student does before publishing.

### 1.10 Soundtrap for Education — DAW collaboration
Sources: https://support.soundtrap.com/hc/en-us/articles/7227609217938-Getting-Started-in-Soundtrap-Guide-for-Teachers , https://www.commonsense.org/education/website/soundtrap , https://campustechnology.com/articles/2016/01/19/soundtrap-releases-education-edition-for-online-music-collaboration.aspx?p=1
- Assignment = template project; each student gets a personal copy with the teacher included → in-progress review.
- Classes as groups; roster import via Google Classroom or CSV; admin screen for groups/students/assignments; feedback by chat or video.

### 1.11 My Music Staff — studio administration (what teachers value)
Sources: https://www.mymusicstaff.com/student-portal/ , https://www.mymusicstaff.com/?p=45855 , https://www.capterra.com/p/148451/my-music-staff/ , https://softwareadvice.com/lms/my-music-staff-profile/reviews , https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode132/
- Note only (out of scope for us): scheduling, self-booking, make-up credits, attendance, invoicing, auto-pay, payroll, birthday messages, lending library. Capterra 4.8 (768 reviews); "all-in-one" praised, glitches criticised.
- Practice side: practice log with timer or manual entry, **notes/questions to teacher**, uploaded practice videos; optional "email teacher" from a log entry; suggested reflection format 3-2-1 (three warm-ups, two areas to improve, one thing done well). Complaint: practice log/video sending "not intuitive".
- One teacher chose MMS for business features though "Tonara did seem like a better student-facing practice app" — teachers split admin and practice tools.

### 1.12 Open Studio (jazz) — online lessons/membership
Sources: https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/open-studio-jazz , https://www.trustpilot.com/review/openstudiojazz.com , https://www.reviews.io/company-reviews/store/open-studio-jazz/questions-and-answers

## 2. General ed-tech benchmarks that set teacher expectations

### Google Classroom
Sources: https://support.google.com/edu/classroom/answer/9093530?hl=en , https://support.google.com/edu/classroom/answer/9335069 , https://pianopantry.com/podcast/episode132/
- Private comments (text, or audio/video/screen up to 5 min on paid tiers); **comment bank** (type # for most-used comments); rubrics created per assignment, **reused from previous assignments**, exported/shared; feedback visible after **Return**; submission history; reply to comments from email.
- **Guardian email summaries** (new work, upcoming deadlines, missing work), frequency chosen by the guardian, off per class.
- Co-teachers per class (standard norm). Used by some private piano teachers after Tonara because it is free and has a "stream" for studio-wide announcements.

### Seesaw
Sources: https://www.commonsense.org/education/articles/teachers-essential-guide-to-seesaw , https://help.seesaw.me/hc/en-us/articles/205803229 , https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id930565184
- **Voice comments** on student work; students reply; **turn work into a draft and send back** for revision; teacher approves posts and family comments before they are visible; private folder for assessments kept from families; family notifications on new posts; translation for families; skill/standard tagging with star ratings (premium).
- Downsides: no gradebook; progress tracking paywalled; moved toward school-wide sales, "less useful for individual teachers". One student: can't play some voice comments.

### Duolingo for Schools
Sources: https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-schools-equitable-learning/ , https://blog.duolingo.com/using-duolingo-in-the-classroom , https://www.schoollibraryjournal.com/story/slj-reviews-duolingo-for-schools-test-drive
- Dashboard columns: **XP, time spent, most recent assignment** per student; assignments under Reports aimed at a "just right" level; on time / late / not done. Older review: "not much detailed student data". Lesson: a game-economy metric (XP) is not what teachers need to diagnose.

### Khan Academy
Sources: https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/360031129891 , https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/38554738905997-What-teacher-reports-are-available-on-Khan-Academy-Reimagined
- Activity overview covers **all work, not only assigned work** (minutes, skills worked on, levelled up, proficient/mastered); Skills tab = skill × student mastery grid with "assigned only" filter; assignment scores CSV; in the new version mastery per Unit Mission.
- Lesson: teachers want to see self-directed learning too, framed by skills, not just assignment completion.

### Quizlet / Kahoot
Not researched in depth; their norms (class reports of most-missed items, live session reports) are reflected in Khan/Duolingo above.

## 3. Research on practice measurement (music pedagogy)
- Duke, Simmons & Cash (2009) "It's Not How Much; It's How": among 17 advanced pianists, practice time and repetition counts did not predict next-day performance; isolating and fixing the exact error location did. https://www.scribd.com/document/494219936/DukeEtAl2 ; summary https://www.structural-learning.com/research-briefings/practise-the-hard-bit
- Barry/UCL and others: quality, not quantity, of practice predicted performance quality at all skill levels. https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1507397/1/Barry2002Practicing151.pdf
- Indiana study of teachers, students and parents: teachers and students do not communicate well about practice time expectations. https://scholarworks.iu.edu/dspace/items/a4c69005-03ed-42d5-90a7-4f8975308e29
- SmartMusic itself admits time tracked in-app undercounts practice; Tonara counted only foreground time. Minutes are a weak, gameable proxy.

## 4. Synthesis

### (a) Features teachers most value, ranked (with evidence)
1. **Reusable assignment library with fast search/tags + templates** — "transformative" (Practice Space), every SmartMusic assignment becomes a template, Practice Prescriptions/Vivid/MetaPractice all centre on it; library migration was the most-praised event after Tonara closed.
2. **Fast assignment creation during the lesson (few clicks, from phone)** — "few clicks needed during lessons" (Vivid), "extra clicks" a complaint (Practice Space), quick in-lesson audio snippet the most-missed Tonara feature.
3. **Hearing the student's actual playing between lessons (recorded takes) and replying quickly** — Practice Space video feed, MMS practice videos, MuseClass, SmartMusic gradebook recordings, Open Studio Feedback Friday. Complaints are about friction (video-only, paid add-on, clunky audio).
4. **Lightweight two-way messaging with audio** — "my favourite thing" (Tonara); "no chat / no audio-only" top Practice Space complaint; Lizbeth/Valerie/Anna valued messaging.
5. **At-a-glance roster: who practised, how often, completion** — Tonara real-time check before the lesson; Vivid frequency stats; Better Practice analytics; Duolingo/Khan columns. Frequency/consistency valued over raw minutes.
6. **Recurring / weekly plans and carry-forward** — MetaPractice repeating drills and automated recurring assignments; Practice Space copy notes forward; Vivid Future/Active tabs; Better Practice spaced-repetition lists and review list; MetaPractice repertoire maintenance rules.
7. **Per-student differentiation inside one class** — studios are 1:1 by nature; MetaPractice levels and per-student routines; Vivid per-student practice modes; SmartMusic tolerance levels and "tempo at least".
8. **Groups / multiple classes and announcements** — Tonara groups by day/exam/level; Practice Space announcements with schedule, pin and read receipts; Google Classroom stream; Better Practice groups.
9. **Parent visibility (summaries, not raw data)** — Better Practice weekly summaries and "started practising" notifications; Google guardian summaries; Seesaw family feed with teacher approval; weekly lesson notes by email (Practice Space).
10. **Reusable rubrics and comment bank for consistent quick feedback** — Google comment bank (#) and reusable rubrics; SmartMusic reusable custom criteria (musicality, evidence of practice, reflection).
11. **Return-for-revision / resubmit loop** — Flat Return + Reset, Seesaw draft send-back, SmartMusic reassign.
12. **Preview as student** — Flat Student Preview, MetaPractice session simulation.
13. **Low/flat, predictable pricing; unlimited students** — Practice Space flat fee praised; Better Practice per-student price and paid recordings criticised. (Note for pricing work.)
14. **Responsive developers / teacher-requested features** — repeatedly praised (Practice Space, Vivid).
15. **Co-teachers/substitutes and data portability** — Better Practice substitutes, Google co-teachers, Practice Space multi-teacher; Tonara import tools.

### (b) Mistakes and complaints to avoid
- **Forcing a time per assignment** (Tonara) — teachers want one daily target or none.
- **Points/leaderboards as the core motivator** — a split audience; some teachers leave for non-gamified tools; "rewards over practice". Make social/points optional per class.
- **Auto-assessment as the grade** — SmartMusic beginners "ready to give up", grades on report cards; MusicFirst districts grade technique manually. Show the machine score as evidence, let the teacher decide.
- **Counting only in-app foreground time** — undercounts and frustrates (Tonara, SmartMusic).
- **Recording/feedback friction**: video-only replies, no audio messages, slow processing, recordings as a paid add-on.
- **Losing the "save to library" chance at publish**, no bulk tagging, tag clouds that scroll.
- **Too many options / overwhelming dashboards** (Better Practice), redesigns that break established workflows ("step backward" SmartMusic).
- **Forced structures** (mandatory grading periods, SmartMusic, removed 2023).
- **Game metrics on the teacher dashboard** (XP) instead of diagnostic data (Duolingo critique).
- **Progress marks that don't reflect reality** (Open Studio checkmarks).
- **Notification sprawl** — MusicFirst consolidated to-grade into one bell and removed duplicate alerts.
- **Paywalling basic progress tracking** (Seesaw) and abandoning individual teachers for school sales.
- **Vendor lock-in / sudden shutdown** — keep export.

### (c) What is unique to MUSIC teaching vs a generic LMS
- **The evidence is sound over time.** The teacher's core job is *listening*: a recorded take, ideally with the machine's per-note/timing analysis next to it, and timestamp-anchored comments (Flat anchors comments to a spot in the score; the music equivalent is a bar/beat or second in a take). Audio reply from the teacher (a sung or played correction) is more natural than text.
- **Progress is a tempo/accuracy curve, not completion.** SmartMusic's "tempo at least", MetaPractice repeating drills, our own measured takes: teachers want to see the tempo ladder rising, cleanliness at each tempo, and which key/passage keeps failing — not "done/not done".
- **Practice quality > minutes.** Research (Duke 2009; Barry) and vendor admissions make minutes a weak proxy; frequency, focused work on trouble spots, and clean-take tempo are better signals.
- **Repetition and maintenance.** Music assignments recur (daily scales, a piece over 6 weeks, repertoire upkeep, all 12 keys). Generic LMS assignments are one-shot; music needs recurring/rolling assignments and review rotation (MetaPractice repertoire rules, Better Practice spaced repetition).
- **The lesson is the anchor event.** Private teachers prepare right before the weekly lesson ("did the student warm up?") and assign during/after it; the weekly lesson note is the canonical artefact. Flows should fit the 2 minutes before and after a lesson.
- **Two different teacher worlds:** (1) **private studio** — 1:1, everyone differentiated, ages 6–70, parents involved for children, teacher is a small business (scheduling/payments live in MMS-type tools); (2) **school ensemble/classroom** — one piece for 30 students, parts per instrument, gradebook and LMS sync, admin compliance. A product should pick its primary world; the private/jazz-coaching studio fits this app's measured 1:1 practice best, with groups as a secondary layer (ensemble/combo, workshop cohort like Open Studio's group feedback).
- **Demonstration media from the teacher** — a teacher playing the passage, a backing track, a recorded lick — is part of the assignment itself, not an attachment.
- **Instrument- and level-specific material** — keys, tempo, hand, voicing, tuning; the same exercise is a different task in E♭ at 120 than in C at 80.
- **Motivation for children vs adults** differs sharply; teachers want to control the gamification dial per class.
- **Jazz specifically:** much of the value is live/group feedback on performance (Open Studio), lick/vocabulary transfer to all keys, and improvisation where "correct" is not binary — teacher judgment and rubric criteria (time feel, vocabulary, voice leading) matter more than auto scores.
