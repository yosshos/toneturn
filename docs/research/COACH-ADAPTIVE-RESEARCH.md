## Tempo ladders

- **[P] Teachers:** +5 bpm after a clean playing, and hold or drop after a stumble.
- **[P] Melodics Auto BPM:** +10 bpm per clean loop.
- **[P] Yousician:** 5% steps between 25% and 125%.
- No controlled study compares step sizes.
- **[E] Duke, Simmons & Cash 2009 (JRME):** among advanced pianists, how errors were handled predicted retention, not
  practice time.
- **[E] Chunking:** Van Vugt, Jabusch & Altenmüller 2012 found that unevenness clusters at the octave boundaries
  (thumb passages).
  - PMC3499913

## Placement

- **[E] Levitt 1971:** a transformed staircase (2-down/1-up) converges on about 71% success.
- **[E] Guadagnoli & Lee 2004:** in the challenge-point framework, the optimal difficulty rises with skill.
- **[E] Wilson et al. 2019:** about 85% success was optimal, shown for simple learners.
- **[I]** Use the self-report as the prior, then 3–5 probe takes with large jumps, then small steps.

## Interleaving

- **[E] Abushanab & Bishara 2013, 25 pianists:** random order was slower while practising but better at the 2-day
  retention test. Players believed the fixed order was better.
- **[E] Carter & Grahn 2016, clarinettists:** the interleaved condition was rated better.
- **[E/I]** Contextual interference helps more as skill grows; beginners do better with blocked practice first.

## Timing

- **[E] Measures:** the SD of the gap between notes (IOI) on a MIDI 2-octave scale (Jabusch 2004).
- **[E] Conservatory pianists at 8 notes/s:** jitter ≈ 9 ms, about 7% of the IOI (Van Vugt 2012).
- **[E] Dystonic professionals:** median IOI SD 14.8 ms at a 187.5 ms IOI (Cheng 2013).
- **[E] Amateurs:** they could not hold a 125 ms IOI and averaged about 142 ms (Winges & Furuya 2014).
  - The main amateur failure at speed is drifting behind the tempo.
- **[E] Hearing threshold for one displaced note:** about 2.5% of the IOI on slow notes (Friberg & Sundberg 1995).

## Apps [P]

- **Melodics:**
  - stars at 50%, 75% and 90% accuracy;
  - Auto BPM;
  - practice loops.
- **Yousician:** auto-speed.
- **Others** (Flowkey, Simply Piano, Skoove, Playground):
  - slow-down presets;
  - wait mode;
  - onboarding self-report.
  - None was found to run a measured, multi-axis technique staircase.

## By key vs by topic

- **[E/P] Exam syllabi** set technique per key: scale, arpeggio, contrary motion, broken chords and a cadence.
- **[P]** Teachers often order keys by fingering family, not by the circle of fifths.
- **[I]** Use "by key" as the session frame and "by topic" as the progression track.
