# Market review — November 2026 (after 0.48)

Three parallel reviews (≈ 100 sources, 2025–26) of ~80 products, compared with the app as described in
`APP_INVENTORY_0.48.md`. Prices and some new-entrant claims are from store listings or aggregators and were not all
verified on vendor pages. Full source lists are in the three agent reports (kept in the session log); key links below.

## 1. The big picture

**Where we stand.** No product combines what we have in one place: a deep generated ear/theory engine (nothing can be
memorised), an adaptive path, voice analysis that names faults, piano/guitar technique with per-note feedback, jazz
pedagogy you are *graded* on (Barry Harris, voicings × comping, LH-comps/RH-improvises), improvisation **scored under
rules** (no commercial product grades improvisation at all), a research-based piece coach, live multiplayer games, and
Hebrew + maqam. Competitors are single-purpose (EarMaster, Melodics, iReal, SRF) or LMS bundles of several vendors
(MusicFirst). Yousician closed its teacher programme and Tonara shut down — the "gamified + teacher" space is open.

**Where the market is going (2025–26).**
1. **Real recordings as content** — Chordify, Moises, Hooktheory, Songsterr AI, Klangio, "Real Ear Trainer": chords,
   stems, tabs and quizzes from the user's own music or YouTube.
2. **AI coaches** — conversational tutors (ROLI AI Music Coach with camera hand tracking, Artie, Yousician AI Guitar
   Teacher, SoundGate, MusPal). None is grounded in a real theory engine; several hallucinate.
3. **Time feel as a profile** — new apps chart early/late per hit and "pocket" over weeks (PocketGroove, Metronome Hero).
4. **Accompaniment that follows you** — Metronaut (score following), Magenta RealTime 2 / Lyria (AI jam), Yamaha Genos2
   AI Chord.
5. **Teacher loop** — assign → student records → auto-grade with strictness → teacher override → LMS passback →
   class analytics (MakeMusic Cloud, SRF, Flat, which in Sept 2026 added ear-training and practice tools).

## 2. By area — what exists, what we lack, what to add

### Ear: intervals, degrees, harmony, form
- **Market:** EarMaster 7 (2,500 exercises, courses built on one famous piece, clap-into-mic), ToneGym (daily workout,
  one "Performance Index", coins → weekly contests), Functional Ear Trainer (a wrong note *resolves to the tonic*),
  Toned Ear ("no reveal — keep guessing"), Hooktheory Chord Crush (chords from real songs, personal confusion sets),
  Chordle (daily theory puzzle), Real Ear Trainer (quizzes from your own music).
- **We lack:** harmony / form heard **in real recordings**; resolve-to-tonic feedback; elimination mode; a visible
  confusion matrix; one headline score; courses built around one piece.
- **Add:** *Form radar* (tap section changes on your own audio / YouTube embed), *Where is home?* (tap when the tonic
  arrives in a real song), *Harmonic GPS* (predict the next degree before it sounds), *Reharm swap* (one chord changed —
  which bar, what function), a confusion map with "boss fights", resolve-to-tonic + keep-guessing modes, a daily
  audio Chordle with share squares.

### Time, rhythm, groove
- **Market:** Melodics (hit-by-hit timing), Perfect Ear (latency complaints!), PocketGroove / Metronome Hero (rush /
  lay-back profile, band sync), KingMetronome (microrhythm).
- **We have:** reading, dictation, tapping, polyrhythm (two hands), swing, inner clock / gap click, tempo memory, rhythm
  highway, Beat Echo.
- **Add:** a **groove fingerprint** (early/late per subdivision and tempo, weekly trend), **pocket targets** (hold a
  chosen offset — lay back 15 ms — against a loop), a **click that fades adaptively** until you drift, a **swing-ratio
  dial** (hear → set → play), **band-sync duels** (score = how together two players are), **clap-in** for every rhythm
  topic with a calibration screen.

### Theory understanding
- **Market:** musictheory.net/Tenuto (free, trusted), Teoria (dictation settings), Hookpad Aria (AI harmoniser),
  LLM tutors (MusPal, GTS Pro routes language to a rule engine).
- **Add:** an **LLM coach grounded in our engine** — explains *why* you missed an item from the engine's facts ("the
  bass moved to 6, so it's vi") and sends you to a 2-minute drill; "explain this song" for your own audio (draft chords,
  you correct bar by bar, each correction becomes a drill); a spoken mistake replay after a session.

### Games
- **Market:** Theta (≈ 50 arcade games), ToneGym contests with coins, Smule duets, Duolingo leagues & hearts.
- **We have more multiplayer than anyone** (duels, ghosts, live rooms, tournaments, bingo, leagues, duet streaks).
- **Add:** *Mystery-song detective* (a PD tune revealed feature by feature), *asymmetric duels* (one writes a rhythm /
  progression, the other dictates it; the writer scores if hard but fair), a coin economy for contest entry, an
  **Ear Index** + seasonal ladders, a yearly "Wrapped".

### Voice
- **Market:** Simply Sing (transposes pop songs to your range), Singing Carrots (diagnostics + outcome data), Vanido
  (daily 3 exercises), Sing Sharp (breath detection), Good Voice Meter / Voitore / Voicelyt (strain, roughness,
  breathiness scores — none clinically validated), Smule (duets).
- **We lead** on fault feedback (scoop, drift, wobble, 11 vibrato kinds, messa di voce, harmony, BH singing).
- **Add:** a **range-fit songbook** (any song auto-transposed to your measured range), a **vocal-health guardian**
  (session load × loudness × range extremes, warm-down prompts, "voice rest day" in the streak — clearly *not
  medical*), breath / sustain metrics, the register-bridge exercise (still on our list), duet singing graded on blend.

### Piano
- **Market:** Simply Piano / flowkey / Skoove (polyphonic mic detection with Fraunhofer), Piano Marvel (SASR normed
  sight-reading score, teacher dashboard), Tomplay (recorded accompaniments), Metronaut (follows you), Pianolympics
  (1,000+ scales/arps with per-hand timing, dynamics, balance), Hanon Pro, ROLI (camera hand tracking).
- **We lack:** a **real score view** with cursor + wait mode; **polyphonic acoustic-piano detection by mic**; iOS MIDI;
  video; a normed sight-reading score.
- **Add:** an **evenness fingerprint** (per-finger velocity / timing variance drawn on a hand → prescribed drill),
  **repertoire-aware ear training** (dictations from the bars of the piece you are learning), **style transfer**
  (play this melody as stride, bossa, Barry Harris block chords — graded), **adaptive harmonic simplification**
  (triads → 7ths → upper structures matched to your voicing level).

### Guitar
- **Market:** Yousician, Fender Play (multi-angle video), JustinGuitar (one-minute changes with chord detection,
  songbook filtered by chords you know), Ultimate Guitar Practice Mode, Tonebridge (tones), Rocksmith+, Songsterr AI
  tabs, Chordify Toolkit (live chord detection), SoundGate / Soundcheck (timing per stroke).
- **We have** a wide technique catalogue, tab views, chord ring-check.
- **Add:** a **songbook filtered by the chords you know**, a **picking-timing map** (early/late per stroke for
  alternate / economy picking), buzz / mute detection on single notes, bend-pitch and vibrato-width scoring in the lead
  family, practise-this-song mode on your own audio with section loops + live chord check.

### Working on pieces, playing by ear, transcription
- **Market:** Moises (stems, chords, smart metronome), Chordify, Klangio (audio → MusicXML), Transcribe!/Anytune,
  Soundslice (PDF scan → synced score), Metronaut, "play by ear" single-skill trainers.
- **We have** the piece coach (unique), slow-down & A–B loop.
- **Add:** the **score view** (phase B of SPEC-PIECE), an **error-pattern memory** (the bars you stumble on across
  sessions become spaced micro-drills), a **"figure it out" mode** on your own audio (find the key, then the bass, then
  the qualities — reveal the AI analysis only as a check), a **melody-to-chart transcription coach** (sing a tune you
  know → the app transcribes → you fix it), **inner hearing before playing** (audiate the next 2 bars, sing the first
  note, then play), practice-quality analytics (deliberate vs run-through time).

### Technique and restricted improvisation
- **Market:** nobody grades improvisation. Impro-Visor (free, old) has grammar-based "trading fours" that answers MIDI;
  research measures feel (Cheston 2024) and creativity proxies.
- **We lead** with rules scored note by note + bar map, 14 levels, LH-comps/RH-improvises.
- **Add:** a **motif-development score** (find a 3–5 note cell, reward its transformed return: transposed, displaced,
  inverted, sequenced — drawn as arcs on the bar map); **phrasing & space** (phrase lengths, rests, density — "max 70 %
  density" as a rule); **arc & contour** ("your peak came in bar 2 — build to bar 7"); **time feel from MIDI** (swing
  ratio, behind / ahead, consistency, and "play laid-back" as a rule); a **vocabulary detector** (enclosures, approach
  notes, BH scale moves, digital patterns); **trading fours with an AI** that develops *your* motif, and scoring whether
  you develop its idea; **responsive comping** (the band reharmonises at higher levels; land on the 3rd of the
  substituted chord); teacher-authored constraint improvisations with a rubric.

### Teaching
- **Market loop:** assign → record → auto-grade with **strictness** (Lenient / Moderate / Strict) → teacher override →
  Google Classroom passback → class heat-map → "items to grade" inbox (MakeMusic Speedy Grading). Flat added ear and
  practice tools in Sept 2026. Charanga: termly learning plans. Piano Marvel: SASR benchmark.
- **We have** classes and assignments of lessons / levels, leagues, bingo.
- **Add (G2 + SPEC-PIECE C):** assign **pieces, charts with improvisation rules, drills and topics** with a strictness
  level and due date; a **grading inbox** across voice / piano / improvisation takes with timestamped comments; a
  **class heat-map** (students × 24 skills); **Google Classroom** passback & roster import; a **benchmark** retaken each
  term (built on the level test); a **parent view** (weekly digest + one recording; ties into young-player mode);
  printable reports and certificates.

### Israel first: Bagrut and conservatories
The music Bagrut (5 units) includes theory, ear training and solfège (8 % internal), meter identification and rhythmic
dictation in every track (art / pop-rock / **jazz** / **Arabic music**), excerpt analysis, and performance (17 %). JAMD,
TAU and Bar-Ilan entrance exams ask for interval / chord / mode work, transposition and clefs, jazz harmonic analysis,
melodic / two-voice / harmonic / rhythmic dictation, and sight-singing with modulation. We already cover almost all of
it; **nobody serves it in Hebrew.** Add: Bagrut mock exams per track, an internal-component portfolio export,
conservatory entrance prep with timed mocks and a readiness report, an exam-day simulation (no replays, strict timer,
"the committee picks 2 of 3 pieces").

## 3. Priorities

**Now (high impact, mostly built on what exists)**
1. **Score view + wait mode** (SPEC-PIECE B) — unlocks pieces, sight-reading and teacher assignments.
2. **Teacher loop**: assign pieces / charts-with-rules / drills with strictness → grading inbox → class heat-map →
   parent digest. Fills the Yousician/Tonara gap.
3. **Bagrut & conservatory mode** — our home market, uncontested.
4. **Quick ear wins**: resolve-to-tonic, keep-guessing, confusion map + boss fights, Ear Index, daily audio Chordle,
   clap-in.
5. **Improvisation beyond notes**: motif development, phrasing & space, time feel — keeps our unique lead.

**Next**
6. **Bring your own music**: form radar, where-is-home, figure-it-out, range-fit songbook — legal (the user brings the
   audio or a YouTube embed + our annotations).
7. **Groove fingerprint, pocket targets, band-sync duels.**
8. **A grounded AI coach** (engine facts → explanation → drill), in Hebrew and English.
9. **Technique fingerprints** (piano evenness hand map, guitar picking-timing map).

**Later (platform)**
10. Polyphonic acoustic-piano detection by mic; native iOS (MIDI); score following / an accompanist that follows;
    AI trading fours; short demo videos (or the 3D avatars) for technique; vocal-health guardian.
