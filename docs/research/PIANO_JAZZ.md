# Jazz piano: Barry Harris and advanced jazz (2026-11-10)

The owner's direction for the keys course: it must be complete, advanced jazz included, before the other courses get
more work. The app's niche is "sophisticated music": practice for jazz players that is **measured** and shows
progress. A player should not just play along to a teacher video.

This round deepens the jazz side of the piano course. It follows `PIANO_AUDIT.md`. All the material is our own,
written in the tradition: every line and voicing is generated from rules (chord tones, scales, voice leading). Nothing
is copied from Open Studio, from a Barry Harris workshop transcript or from a book.

## 3. What was built

### `harris` 14–15 (lines)

- **14 — the half-step rules from any start.** The line goes down the scale with a chord tone on every beat. Between
  two chord tones goes the one scale tone between them. Where none fits, a chromatic tone goes there: root → ♮7 → ♭7
  on a dominant, 6 → ♭6 → 5 on a 6th chord. From a tension the half step comes at once (C7 from the 9th: D D♭ C B B♭
  A G …). Items: C7 from the 9th, C7 from the 13th, C6 from the 9th, Cm6 (melodic minor) from the 9th, and the LH.
- **15 — ii–V–I lines by the rules.** Each chord: an arpeggio up from a chord tone, then the scale down by the rules
  from the next chord tone above. In minor: Dø is played as Fm6, G7♭9 comes down its diminished (half-whole), and
  the tonic is Cm6.

### `k-sixdim`: Barry Harris harmony (9 levels; symbol: a block chord beside the diminished circle)

| Level | Items | What is measured |
|---|---|---|
| 1 The scale of chords from every chord tone | melody from 1, 3, 5, 6, up an octave and back (17 chords) | each chord's pitch classes (any voicing in its set), voice leading, timing |
| 2 Borrowing | each voice in turn steps to the nearest diminished note (a half step where there is one) and back | as above |
| 3 Oblique motion | LH holds the root (or root + 5th) a bar; RH three voices move through the scale | as above, LH held |
| 4 Spreads | 2nd and 4th voices down an octave into the LH; or the melody in octaves in the RH | as above, hands together |
| 5 Diminished passing chords | I6 – ♯I°7 – ii7 – ♯ii°7 – I6/3 – IV6 – ♯IV°7 – I6/5 – VI7 – ii7 – V7 – I6, and a descending version | as above |
| 6 Minor 6th-diminished | inversions (from 1 and 5) and borrowing (melody on 1 and 6) | as above |
| 7 The dominant ♭9 from the diminished | B°7 → raise one note a whole step → 7♭9 on it → its 6th chord, all four keys | as above |
| 8 ii–V–I the 6th-diminished way | major: F6/E°7 over D, Dm6/C♯°7 over G, C6/B°7; minor: Fm6/E°7, G7 ↔ A♭°7, Cm6/B°7 | as above |
| 9 A standard's form | the ii–V–I cycle standard and rhythm changes A: each chord as 6th chord → diminished → 6th chord | as above, with the chart above the staff |

No accents are written, so every level counts by microphone too (the chroma path, pitch-class sets).

### `k-bebop`: bebop lines (8 levels; symbol: four beamed 8ths running down to a landing)

The lines come from `bebopLine()`, which is seeded:

- a chord tone on every beat;
- the next chord tone up or down, with the scale tone between them (chromatic where none fits);
- a half step or step into each new chord's guide tone (3rd or 7th);
- on option 2, triplet turns on a chord's first beat;
- the last guide tone held.

The leaps stay within a 6th.

| Level | Forms | Tempo ♩ |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ii–V–I | from the top / from below / with triplets | 80 → 140 |
| 2 minor ii–V–i | iiø – V7♭9 (harmonic-minor 5th mode) – i m6 | 80 → 140 |
| 3 ii–V7alt–I | the altered scale; targets 3, ♭7, ♭9, ♯9, ♭13 | 80 → 140 |
| 4 jazz blues (12 bars, with ii–Vs and ♯IV°) | | 90 → 160 |
| 5 rhythm changes A | | 100 → 180 |
| 6 standards: the ii–V–I cycle; a minor standard (12 bars) | | 90 → 160 |
| 7 both hands: LH shells (root + 3rd or 7th, the nearer one), RH line | ii–V–I, the blues | 70 → 120 |
| 8 trading fours | ii–V–I, the blues, rhythm changes | 90 → 140 |

These lines use Barry's judgement, like the Harris family. With every beat's chord tone right, up to 10 % of other
notes may differ: small changes to a line are allowed.

**Trading fours (new measurement, `goal.free`).** The take is 16 bars. The app's piano plays four bars (a bebop
line over LH shells) and the player answers with four bars of their own, twice.

- **Landings.** The player's bars are free. Their written notes are landings (`ExpNote.land`) on beats 1 and 3 of
  each bar: any chord tone of that chord, any octave, within a quarter beat. The landings' timing feeds the usual
  timing SD and outlier rules.
- **Other notes.** The rest of the player's notes are not matched note for note. They must lie in the chord's scale,
  or a half step into the next note that does; at most 15 % may fall outside.
- **Density.** Each bar needs at least two notes, so laying out doesn't pass.
- **The band's call.** The band's phrase stops a beat before the player's bars. A speaker heard by the microphone
  (+120 ms tail) is never taken for the answer.
- **Screen.** The card shows Listen / You bars with each bar's chords. The notes appear only with the result.

### `k-reharm`: jazz-piano harmony (7 levels; symbol: one chord turning into another)

All levels are two-handed and voice-led, built by `twoHand()`. Each chord is judged by its pitch classes; the
chord's tensions are allowed as extra notes.

| Level | Items |
|---|---|
| 1 Altered dominants in ii–V–I | LH 3 & 7 voice-led; RH on V: ♭VI (7♯9♭13), ♭V (7♭9♯11), ♭III minor (7♯9♯11), 13♭9; Dm11, C6/9 around it; twice |
| 2 Minor ii–V–i in two hands | ø11 – 7♭9♭13 – m6/9, and ø11 – 7♯9♭13 – m(maj9) |
| 3 Ballad voicings | LH 10ths (or root–7th), RH four notes; I–vi–ii–V, iii–VI–ii–V–I; ♩ 50 → 72 |
| 4 Reharmonising | tritone subs (E♭7 for A7, D♭7 for G7); the backdoor iv7–♭VII7 against ii–V; passing diminished; a ii before every dominant |
| 5 Modal comping over a pedal | three-note 4ths through dorian on 1, the "and" of 2, 4; the mode's triads (♭III, IV, v, ii, ♭VII) over a pedal |
| 6 Chains | ii–Vs falling by whole steps into a new key (Countdown-type); the family of dominants falling by half steps (E7 E♭7 D7 D♭7 → C, then on to F) |
| 7 Solo piano | walking LH quarters (root, 3rd, 5th, half step into the next root) with RH guide tones on 1 and the "and" of 2; LH 10ths with an RH chord-tone line; two-feel (bass 1 & 3, rootless chords 2 & 4) |

### Tests

`tests/pianoJazz.test.ts` (15) checks:

- **Content:** every item in all 12 keys (pitch range; the hands never meet); the half-step rule note for note; the
  scale of chords; borrowing; the 7♭9 from the diminished; chord tones on every beat of the bebop lines, with leaps
  within a 6th.
- **Trading fours:** the windows; another chord tone, an octave up, passes; a wrong landing, laying out, or a line
  outside the changes fails.
- **The sweep:** every item of every new level in all 12 keys at the target tempo. A steady take (jitter 5–9 ms) is
  clean, and a sloppy one (2 × the SD limit) never is (the 3–5-chord rule of `PIANO_AUDIT` A3). The microphone case
  is also checked: chord and line levels are clean by mic.
- **Wiring:** emphasis, symbols, path order, badges, rating tags, and the topics with their lessons.

`tests/pianoAudit.test.ts`, `pianoEar.test.ts` and `badges.test.ts` pass with the new families included (unique
symbols, Hebrew titles without English words, the A3 sweep).

## 4. Decisions for the owner

1. **Trading fours by rules.** Your bars are free. Beats 1 and 3 must be chord tones, ≤ 15 % of your notes may fall
   outside the scale, and you need at least two notes a bar. Stricter options: target 3rds and 7ths only, or land on
   the next chord's guide tone at each change. Looser options: beat 1 only. Which?
2. **Bebop line leniency.** As with Harris lines, a line passes with up to 10 % of notes changed when every beat's
   chord tone is right. Keep it, or require note for note?
3. **Path order.** k-sixdim comes after Harris level 5 or so (+9); bebop and harmony come after the guide tones and
   rootless voicings (+6). Should the jazz emphasis bring them even earlier?
4. **Tempos.** Rhythm changes aim at ♩ 180 and blues / standards at 160 (swung 8ths). Are those the right targets
   for "done"?
