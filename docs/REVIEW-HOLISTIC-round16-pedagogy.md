# Round 16 — independent content/pedagogy review (SPEC-HOLISTIC-2 v12 + §19–§23; SPEC-SPIRAL-PRACTICE v6 + §21–§22)

## (1) Verdict: CLEAN

I found no substantive problem in the content and pedagogy. Nothing here would make a builder build the wrong thing or teach a learner the wrong thing, provided the §19–§23 binding changes are merged as written.

## (2) Substantive issues

None.

What I checked:
- **Coverage.** Every AP top-15 item and every §3 cross-cutting gap in AUDIT-HOLISTIC-PEDAGOGY maps to a section. That includes the repertoire spiral (§8.5), the tune gate (§13.1), goal-tune onboarding (§6.1), the performance ritual (§12.4), ensemble roles, expression and how-to-practise.
- **Sequencing.** These all follow sound jazz pedagogy:
  - learn-a-tune order: listen, sing, melody, roots, guide tones, shells, memory, keys, solo;
  - band-1 variants;
  - prerequisite gating that skips a step and never locks it;
  - story-ladder order;
  - accompaniment levels by name (§21);
  - burst, LH and register-jump rungs in SP.
- **Grading criteria.** Each one is musically valid, or is labelled descriptive, "needs MIDI", or "teacher or recording needed":
  - swing band by tempo, with ballads widening upward (§13.4, §19);
  - cents on steady-state mean pitch;
  - register overlap and phrase-end density for comping;
  - release-on-cue fermata;
  - buzz-roll quality;
  - "tunes you know" with the octave free and the key fixed (§21).
- **Legal facts.** I checked the 1A beginner and core heads against STANDARDS_LEGAL death years and print years (Indiana, Rose Room, Avalon, Japanese Sandman, Ain't We Got Fun, Charleston, If I Could Be with You, Love Me or Leave Me, Mean to Me, My Blue Heaven, Bill Bailey, Somebody Stole My Gal, Nobody Knows You). The latest-author rule (§19) puts every co-written candidate in the right tier: Honeysuckle Rose and Ain't Misbehavin' (Razaf, 1973), Sweet Georgia Brown, Tea for Two and What Is This Thing Called Love are all 1B. These are also correct:
  - Handy (1958) is 1B; Pixinguinha is 1A only from 2044;
  - the US terms: compositions published ≤ 1930 and recordings ≤ 1925 are PD as of 2026;
  - §303(a)/(b): an unpublished work by an author who died ≤ 1955 is PD; publication in 1978–2002 gives protection to 2047; a work made for hire runs 120 years from creation;
  - Baby Dodds died 1959;
  - the contrafact pairs, and the PD side alone gating each card.
- **Project rules.** The rules below are all respected. None of the content adds Barry Harris material, and SP §14 excludes unconfirmed Harris claims.
  - **No Claude-composed music:** the lick-bank soloist plays licks as-is, only when they fit the changes, and falls back to drums or silence; rubato and model curves come from human takes only; Contour Draw is random stimulus.
  - **No movable do:** the syllable rule allows a neutral syllable, degree numbers or letters, fixed do included.
  - **No locks:** skippable capstones; entry limits only the planner.
  - **Hands-free and one screen:** the §7 tap and the embed click are named as the only exceptions; scores show in 4–8-bar windows.

## (3) Minor notes (optional)

1. **Merge hygiene, so the builder doesn't build the weaker recording test.** §8.1 (b)/(c), the §8.10 phrase-flag wording and §18 step 5 ("the recording test (a)–(c), every improvising ensemble member") still describe the pre-§22/§23 test. That test misses arrangers, work-for-hire and 1978–2002 publication. The same applies to the §8.2 Ain't Misbehavin' note (Brooks rather than Razaf) and the Groove Match rows in §1.10 and §13.2, which §20 makes a game. The overrides are explicit, but these should be the first edits at the v13 merge.
2. **§6.1 "with a singer" goals.** Completion needs a human singer's take (§12.3). The human model bank (§8.9) covers only 1A heads, so a self-learner with no studio singer can set a goal on a by-ear standard (the "Autumn Leaves with a singer" example) that can never complete. Offer "with a singer" only when a studio singer, or a bank take for that head, exists.
3. **Memory step at band 3+ (§8.4 step 7).** Timing is loosened, but pitch is graded against the "as printed" melody, so idiomatic embellishment would read as an error. Consider grading structural notes at band 3+ (the paraphrase DTW alignment already exists), or say in the brief that the memory step is "as printed".
4. **Voice "sung roots" (§8.4 voice variant).** State that octave displacement into voiceRange is accepted, as for the instrumentalists' singing step. Otherwise low roots will be out of range for most voices.
5. **Voice steps 2 and 3 both sing the melody.** Give the sequence explicitly: syllable or degree-number pass, then the lyric pass (§19).
