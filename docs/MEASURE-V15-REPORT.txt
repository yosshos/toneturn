# Measurement harness report, SPEC-HOLISTIC-2 v15 defaults
seed 20261127. Defaults: item pass V1 (+ a pending pair expires after 60 days); rank = H8 (>= 2 exercises, grouped tiers, keys = union) + tier guard G (bar 0.5, P >= 0.8, half-life 24 checks, >= 6 checks; the guard reserve);
round 18 (v15): cells unique per exercise x key (shelved cells resumed, never duplicated); at a tier with no fresh cell left the guard also reads the passed cells' probe-band looks, and the reserve plays a guard probe there;
a skipped due check (cell sounded before / without its due cold check) is a miss (guard + V1 share); due checks left unplayed are misses while the skip share over 30 days > 0.2;
H10: evidence readiness (>= 2 clean of the last <= 3 probe-band cold takes, or a non-provisional threshold >= 0.95), content edge capped at the entered top, struggle step-down (probe-band p estimate < 0.25 over 21 days and not rising, lifts at >= 0.5);
current form rho_d 0.15, rho_c 0.2 (cautious); supply = 3 slots per session shared by 4 areas + FSRS-review looks (<= 2 per session per area); N1 over all cells at placedTier.
Learners: day SD 0.8, cell SD 0.7 (logit), beta 1.2 logit per tier, warm +0.8 logit.
Truths: "probe-band p" = cold p on a FRESH cell of the tier at the floor of the probe band (0.95 x target in tempo areas, the fixed tempo in fixed-tempo areas) — the RANK ESTIMAND (v15, §1.1);
"tier p at target" = the same at 1.0 x target; "catalogue p" = mean over every exercise x key of the tier with that cell's own item-practice gain (unoffered cells at 0);
"practised p" = the recency-weighted mean probe-band p of the cells behind the guard's record, with their item practice (what the guard actually measured).

Sections were run in parallel processes (`npm run measure-v15 -- --only=<section>`), each with its own seeds as in the script; the "(N s)" lines are per-section run times.
