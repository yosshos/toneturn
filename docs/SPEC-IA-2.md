## What was wrong

- **Practice hub (phone):** four big cards (Songs, Technique, Games, Lessons) are followed by one flat row of ten "tools". That row mixes real tools (tuner, metronome) with whole features (improvisation, programs & exams, charts, the daily progression). The features most worth finding look like small utilities.
- **Sidebar (computer):** after 0.53 it has two top-level features (Improvise, Programs & exams) beside a "Practice" section that holds six others. There is no single logic.
- **Phone menu:** it repeats some of these in a "Learn" group.
- **Leftovers:** emoji on the Play cards (ghost race, duel, daily, online room), the big gradient puzzle hero, and the "Practice" title above the hub.
- **Sound setting:** eight equal buttons in one grid. That does not scale to the new instruments and says nothing about the band (drums, bass).

## The model: four verbs

Everything a player does outside the path and Today falls into one of four groups:

| Group | Hebrew | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| **Play** — real music | לנגן | Songs · My pieces · Charts & lead sheets |
| **Train** — skills | להתאמן | Technique (instrument and voice) · Improvise · Games |
| **Study** — knowledge and goals | ללמוד | Lessons · Programs & exams · Progression of the day |
| **Tools** | כלים | Tuner · Metronome · Drone · Groove trainer · Slow down · Mixer · Practice log |

## Practice tab (phone) = the same four groups

Each group is a section with a small heading. Its items are rows, one tap each, with:

- a line icon;
- a title;
- one line of what it is;
- a status on the trailing side, where one exists: pieces in progress, today's progression solved, the next exam section, songs you know.

Tools are a compact grid of icon buttons, four across.

Games open their list, as now. Inside it:

- the gradient puzzle hero becomes a plain row;
- the emoji become line icons;
- the online room becomes a plain row.

## Sound settings, by category

The instrument picker is grouped:

| Category | Instruments |
|---|---|
| Pianos | Concert grand (16 layers) · Upright · Synth piano |
| Electric pianos | Wurlitzer (recorded) · Tine electric piano (modelled) |
| Organs | Tonewheel organ (modelled: drawbars, percussion, rotating speaker) · Pipe organ |
| Mallets & plucked | Vibraphone · Marimba · Harp · Harpsichord |

The chosen instrument shows its own options below the picker:

- **Grand:** detail.
- **Tonewheel organ:** registration, and rotating speaker slow / fast.
- **Tine piano:** tremolo.

A separate **Band** card covers backing tracks and grooves:

- **Drums:** Jazz kit (recorded, 4 dynamics, alternating strikes) or the earlier kits.
- **Bass:** Upright pizzicato, with 3 dynamics and 4 strikes.
