Hello,

I'm the developer of The 7th Sense, a web app (PWA) for measured music practice — ear training, piano technique and jazz study, where players practise with a MIDI keyboard or the microphone and the app measures their playing. Our niche is jazz and "sophisticated" music: Barry Harris harmony, comping, bebop lines and standards with a generated band.

Piano in 162 is the most musical free grand we have heard, and it would suit our jazz material far better than what we use now. Because the app will be a paid product, I'd like to ask before using it:

1. May we ship a trimmed subset of Piano in 162 inside the app (for example 4–8 velocity layers, shortened release tails, re-encoded to a compressed format, streamed to the user's browser and cached on the device)? Users could only play it inside the app; the samples would not be downloadable or usable elsewhere.
2. If that needs a commercial licence, what would the terms and cost be — and would you consider a revenue-share or a fixed fee suited to an early-stage product?
3. Credit: we'd gladly credit Ivy Audio and link to your site in the app's sound settings and about page.

Happy to share more about the app or a demo build. Thank you for making such a beautiful instrument available.
