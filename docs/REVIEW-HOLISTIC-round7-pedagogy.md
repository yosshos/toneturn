# Round 7, pedagogy / content review (HOLISTIC-2 v8 + SP v6)

## (1) Verdict: NOT CLEAN

Coverage is now broad and close to complete. The sequencing is sound (sing before play, melody before roots, paraphrase as the first step of the improv ladder). The legal frame is consistent with H1–H7, and the region gate, the §303(b) improviser rule and the 1A list hold up. Six substantive problems remain. None is an open owner or lawyer question.

## (2) Substantive issues

**M1. The "about 2–3 weeks per tier" figure contradicts the spec's own 2-look cap** (§0 Rank speed, §1.7, §1.10). *Major.*
- The round-5 median of 76 looks at p = 0.75 assumed 4–6 cells a day. §1.7 now counts at most 2 looks per area per day, so the median is at least 38 practice days in that area. Areas are not practised every day, so that means roughly 6–10 weeks per tier. At p = 0.85 it is about 13 practice days.
- The harness can only make this slower, because it may raise the threshold above 0.975.
- The owner approved the rank speed on the wrong figure. Also, promotion effectively needs about 26 of 30 clean (≈ 87 %), so displayed rank sits well below the defined level (p ≥ 0.7, §1.1).
- **Fix:** correct the §0 and §1.10 figures now, using simple arithmetic (≥ 38 practice days per tier per area at p = 0.75). Say plainly that rank measures "p ≥ 0.7 shown with high confidence", which in practice means about 85 % clean. Put the corrected numbers to the owner now, not only after the harness. Levers that keep the rule's form: count 3 looks per day, or count looks per cell rather than per area.

**M2. The guaranteed supply of looks cannot be built as written** (§1.7, §5). *Major.*
- The rule: "while a candidate tier's posterior is open, ≥ 2 cold confirmation slots per practice day" in that area. Below T8, the candidate tier above high-water is always open.
- That makes 7 areas × 2 = 14 forced cold slots a day for a pianist (5 for a bassist). Each must be cold (≥ 1 h since any take in its cell). This swamps a 20–30-minute time-first session, the workout2 quotas and the key of the week.
- **Fix:** cap confirmation slots per day across all areas (e.g. 2–4). Rotate areas by need and goal. Give slots only to areas practised that day, or to posteriors inside a decision band.

**M3. Tune-based families count toward level on memorised material** (§1.10, §1.6). *Major.*
- §1.10 maps learn-a-tune steps to the line and harmony areas, and pop levels and accompaniment to harmony. A learner who repeats the same few heads gives "cold" confirmations that measure memory of those tunes, not skill.
- This breaks the no-memorizable-items rule. §13.5 guards against it for Ear only.
- **Fix:** learn-a-tune steps on a head the learner has already played go to coverage (and `repertoire-memory`). Only transfer forms feed level: a new key at sight (step 8), or a chart at sight. State the same for k-pop and k-accomp-voice. Add a guard test.

**M4. The contrafact pairs can never be shown** (§8.4). *Major.*
- §8.4 says each pair is "shown only where both sides can be served". Hot House, Donna Lee, Anthropology, Scrapple and Dig are all protected, and under H7 their melodies are never served anywhere. Read literally, the feature never appears.
- The Scrapple note then makes it depend on two 1B melodies, which adds to the confusion.
- **Fix:** define what a pair shows: the PD head's melody (region-gated), the bebop tune's chart only, an official embed, and a by-ear option. The gate then depends only on the PD side.

**M5. The by-ear variant has no reference chart to grade against** (§8.4 vs §8.3 / H7). *Major.*
- Roots, guide tones, shells and "chords from memory" are graded, and they decide goal completion. But H7 requires many variations per standard, and the learner learns from a recording whose changes may match none of them.
- **Fix:** the learner or teacher picks one variation as "my chart", and that is the reference. Grading accepts functional equivalents (tritone sub for V7, ii–V for V, inversions, chord extensions). State this in §8.4 and §6.1.

**M6. Swing-native items cannot give cold or level evidence** (SP §16.5 vs HOLISTIC §1.4, §1.6 and §13.4, and SP A1). *Major.*
- SP §16.5 says only straight takes are cold or pass takes. But A1 runs guide tones and shells swung, §13.4 applies the swing band app-wide, and §8.2 accepts swung dotted pairs.
- In a jazz-first app the whole line-jazz area would then confirm only from straight playing.
- **Fix:** define the item's **native feel** (swing for `spec.swing` and jazz-native items). A take in the native feel is the "straight" take for coldness, passes and confirmations. Feel-layer modifiers stay non-qualifying.

## (3) Minor notes
- **Stale pointer:** SP §22 cites "HOLISTIC §19.8, §20", which no longer exist in v8. SP §14's definition of the area tier ("highest tier whose scale benchmark…") is not listed as superseded in §1.16 or §22. Fix both in the pointer commit, which the done criterion requires.
- **Ambiguity:** §1.7 offers unpassed cells "near the band" as confirmation slots, but the §1.6 table requires the probe band (0.95–1.0 × target). Use one definition.
- **Reading supply:** reading confirmations need a steady stream of unseen first-sight items (a window of 30, 3 clean takes on ≥ 2 days per tier). State that reading items are rule-generated per tier (random stimulus, like Contour Draw), or a finite catalogue will run out.
- **§6.1 example:** "Autumn Leaves with a singer" cannot be graded. There is no served melody, and the embed cannot be analysed (H3). Define its completion as needing a human singer's take (§12.3), or change the example.
- **Ballad capstone:** §8.6's capstone "on a 1A ballad" has few candidates (If I Could Be with You, spirituals). Name them, or allow the labelled practice ballad Quiet Hours.
- **Drums:** the drums areas have no time/tempo-stability area, though time is a drummer's core skill. Fold timing SD into grooves explicitly, or add a time area.
- **Voice:** a "range/agility" level can reward pushing range. Tie it to comfortable tessitura and agility, not extremes (vocal-health rule).
- **Owner tasks:** PD repertoire (§14) needs note-by-note verification too. Add it to the §17.5 owner tasks and the time budget.
- **Bar to next:** the posterior bar can move backward after misses. Say so in the display rules, or show a smoothed value, to keep promotion-not-full-bars honest.
- **Non-US regions:** there is no melody head for rhythm changes, modal or Coltrane forms; there they are learned only through the by-ear variant. This is acceptable, but should be stated.
